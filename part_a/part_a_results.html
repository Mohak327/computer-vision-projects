From noisy images to guided edits, visual anagrams, and hybrids.

Part A explores pretrained DeepFloyd diffusion models through forward noising, classical and learned denoising, iterative sampling, classifier-free guidance, image-to-image translation, inpainting, visual anagrams, and hybrid-image generation.

What To Notice

Early sections compare simple Gaussian denoising against learned denoising and show why iterative reverse diffusion works better than a single denoising step.

CFG improves sample quality, while increasing `i_start` in the translation sections allows larger edits to the original image.

The later sections push the same diffusion machinery into more creative tasks: inpainting, prompt-guided edits, visual anagrams, hybrid images, and optional upsampling experiments.
